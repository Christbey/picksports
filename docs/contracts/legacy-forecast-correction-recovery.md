# Legacy forecast correction and recovery

Current persisted legacy grading may append immutable correction receipts while preserving original forecast inputs. Grading and correction recheck final state, complete scores and identity under game-first locks. Unchanged repeats do no work; A-to-B-to-A appends separate revisions. Imported old graded state is explicitly an unknown legacy baseline, not proof of facts observed at its historical grade time.

Historical repair is explicit, bounded and dry-run by default. Require a supported sport and reviewed positive game IDs; apply rechecks current admission. There is no automatic all-history backfill. Ambiguous nullable-version evaluation identities refuse rather than choosing or deleting a duplicate. Earlier valid games may commit before a later per-game refusal. Preserve receipts on rollback and drain incompatible old graders before relying on completeness.
