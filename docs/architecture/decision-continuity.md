## Policy evolution and unfulfilled goals

Use the current [versioning/deprecation policies](../api/v2/README.md) for published operational rules. Their 180-day target/exception process and enum-addition policy are later refinements of the original ADR; a compatibility label does not prove a supported generated client's unknown-enum behavior. Before declaring API acceptance, resolve any contradictory contract, compile the actual supported clients or record a concrete supported-scope decision, and preserve explicit migration/retirement evidence. This continuity record does not silently waive those requirements or claim every historical proposal belongs in today's executable application.

Old release counts, deployment IDs, service-sizing assumptions and staged commands remain historical evidence retrievable from Git. They must not be copied into current runbooks as active infrastructure instructions. Current target verification and release-bound evidence take precedence over an old example while the retained architectural constraints remain traceable.
