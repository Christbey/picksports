# MLB observed-start provenance

Critical timing and point-in-time consumers require complete, current, identity-bound observed start context. Unknown, partial, stale or mismatched evidence is declined. Historical mixed wall-clock rows remain explicitly unverified; a descriptive legacy date may be shown without promoting its provenance.

Do not backfill start epochs, observation clocks, capture UUIDs, quote/weather certification or approval from current mutable projections. Authoritative epochs and legacy SQL/session timestamps have distinct contracts; lossy repeated-hour or unsupported representations may refuse. Apply compatible additive schema before new readers/writers, preserve evidence on rollback and separately qualify fleet compatibility, scale and source availability.
