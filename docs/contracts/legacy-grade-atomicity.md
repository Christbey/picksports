# Legacy grading atomicity boundary

Legacy graders admit established final-status aliases with complete scores. Each candidate is reloaded under game-first then prediction locking reads; grade fields, supplementary updates and evaluation evidence share the prediction connection transaction. A recorder failure rolls the unit back and preserves retry eligibility. Sport-specific score and clock conventions remain owning contracts.

Local SQL atomicity does not establish upstream result revision order, external cache consistency or deployed-worker readiness. Correction history is governed by the separate legacy forecast correction contract. Do not silently reconstruct missing historical observations or treat a grading receipt as a wager or model-validation certificate.
