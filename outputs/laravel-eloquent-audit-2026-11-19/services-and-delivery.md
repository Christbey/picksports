Laravel constructs notifications with domain payloads, so constructor-injecting services into queued notification payloads is not the right default; resolve through framework-injected methods/factories where available.

## Explicitly retain / do not count as cleanup defects

- Player leaderboard SUM/COUNT expressions, InjuryImpactScorer and PlayerPositionGradeService SQL window functions, and historical archive/snapshot query-builder access are appropriate reporting use; forcing those through Eloquent relationship traversal adds cost.
- SportEventIdentitySynchronizer changing sport_event_id and loaded sportEvent is persistence synchronization, not response-only model pollution.
- PlayerPropAnalyzer assigning a previously fetched real game relation and NflPropShadowService assigning a lockForUpdate-loaded game avoid duplicate reads and preserve lock semantics; setRelation alone is not a defect.
- Alert reservation locks/deduplication, notification shouldSend fresh user/template/forecast checks, and transactional delivery accounting are correctness safeguards, not redundant queries to remove.
- Pure PHP normalization, statistical calculations, simulations, scoring loops, bounded collection grouping after batch reads, and typed read-model objects are useful domain code; Laravel does not replace that logic.
- Runtime Schema guards may support compatibility/bootstrapping; remove only after verifying production migration/feature guarantees. No blanket proposal to remove them.

## Validation suggestions

Before changing query behavior, add focused query-growth assertions for digest recipients, alert opportunities and prop batches; prove counts stay bounded as rows scale. Assert payload parity and exact point-in-time quote/Elo selection. No tests executed because this was read-only inspection.
